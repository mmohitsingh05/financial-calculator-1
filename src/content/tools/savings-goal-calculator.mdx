A **savings calculator** helps you work backward from your financial goals to figure out exactly how much to save each month. Whether you're building an emergency fund, saving for a down payment, planning a vacation, or accumulating wealth for retirement, knowing your required monthly savings amount turns an abstract goal into a concrete, actionable plan.

## How to Use This Calculator

Enter your savings goal (the total amount you want to accumulate), the number of years until you need the money, and the expected annual return on your savings. Choose whether you're starting from zero or have an existing balance. The calculator will tell you exactly how much to save each month to reach your goal. You can also compare different scenarios — saving more per month for a shorter period versus saving less over a longer time.

## How It's Calculated

This calculator solves the annuity formula for the payment amount needed to reach a target future value:

**PMT = FV × (r/n) / [ (1 + r/n)^(n×t) − 1 ]**

Where **FV** is your savings goal, **r** is the annual return rate, **n** is the number of savings periods per year (typically 12 for monthly), and **t** is the time in years. If you have an existing balance, it's first projected forward using the compound interest formula, and the remaining savings needed is split across your regular contributions.
