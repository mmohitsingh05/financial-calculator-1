Real estate typically yields 8–15% annualized depending on location and strategy. For business investments or marketing campaigns, many aim for 200–500% or higher.   - question: ROI vs. annualized return — what's the difference?

An **ROI calculator** helps you evaluate the profitability of any investment or business decision. Return on Investment is one of the most widely used financial metrics because it's simple to understand and applies to virtually any situation — from stock investments to real estate deals to marketing campaigns. This calculator shows both total ROI and annualized return so you can compare opportunities fairly.

## How to Use This Calculator

Enter your initial investment amount (include all costs). Then enter the final value of the investment and the number of years you held it. The calculator will show your total ROI percentage, total profit or loss, and the annualized return (CAGR). Use the comparison mode to evaluate multiple investments side by side and see which delivers the best return.

## How It's Calculated

**ROI (%) = [ (Final Value − Initial Investment) / Initial Investment ] × 100**

For multi-year holdings, the annualized ROI (CAGR) is calculated as:

**Annualized ROI (%) = [ (Final Value / Initial Investment)^(1/t) − 1 ] × 100**

Where **t** is the number of years. CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) gives you the year-over-year growth rate, making it possible to fairly compare investments held for different time periods.
