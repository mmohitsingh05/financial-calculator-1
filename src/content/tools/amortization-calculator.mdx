An **amortization calculator** provides complete transparency into how your loan works by generating a full payment-by-payment schedule. Unlike a simple payment calculator that only shows your monthly payment, this tool reveals exactly how much of each payment goes toward principal versus interest, how your loan balance declines over time, and how much total interest you'll pay — critical information for anyone with a mortgage, auto loan, or personal loan.

## How to Use This Calculator

Enter your loan amount, interest rate, and loan term in years or months. The calculator will generate a complete amortization table showing every payment period — typically monthly — with columns for payment number, payment amount, principal portion, interest portion, and remaining balance. You can scroll through the full schedule or jump to a specific year. The summary also shows total interest paid over the entire loan term.

## How It's Calculated

First, the monthly payment is calculated using the standard formula:

**M = P × [ i(1+i)^N ] / [ (1+i)^N − 1 ]**

Then for each period k from 1 to N:

- Interest_k = Remaining_Balance_(k-1) × i
- Principal_k = M − Interest_k
- Remaining_Balance_k = Remaining_Balance_(k-1) − Principal_k

Where **i** is the monthly interest rate (annual rate / 12) and **N** is the total number of payments. The schedule continues until the remaining balance reaches zero. For a 30-year mortgage, that's 360 rows — each showing the gradual shift from interest-heavy payments to principal-heavy ones.
