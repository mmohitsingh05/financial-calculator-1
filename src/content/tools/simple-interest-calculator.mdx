A **simple interest calculator** helps you quickly determine the interest cost or earnings on any loan or investment where interest is calculated only on the principal. Simple interest is commonly used for short-term loans, some auto loans, and certain bonds. Understanding simple interest is the foundation for grasping more complex financial concepts, and this calculator makes the math straightforward.

## How to Use This Calculator

Enter the principal amount (the initial sum of money), the annual interest rate as a percentage, and the time period in years. The calculator will instantly show the total interest earned or owed, and the total amount (principal plus interest). You can adjust any input to see how changes affect the outcome, making it easy to compare different loan or investment scenarios.

## How It's Calculated

Simple interest uses a straightforward formula:

**Interest = P × r × t**

**Total = P + Interest**

Where **P** is the principal amount, **r** is the annual interest rate expressed as a decimal (e.g., 5% = 0.05), and **t** is the time in years. Unlike compound interest, there is no "interest on interest" effect — each period's interest is based solely on the original principal, making the calculation simple and predictable.
