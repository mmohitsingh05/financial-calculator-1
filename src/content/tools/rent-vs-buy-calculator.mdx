A **rent vs buy calculator** helps you make one of the biggest financial decisions of your life with data, not emotion. The choice between renting and buying is about more than just comparing a mortgage payment to rent — it involves property taxes, insurance, maintenance, the opportunity cost of your down payment, and most importantly, how long you plan to stay. This calculator compares the true total cost of each path over your expected time horizon.

## How to Use This Calculator

Enter the home price you're considering, your down payment percentage, expected mortgage rate, and loan term. Enter the monthly rent for a comparable property, your expected annual rent increase, and your investment return rate (for opportunity cost calculations). Enter how many years you plan to stay. The calculator will compare the total cost of renting versus buying over that period, show you the break-even year, and break down all the costs for each path.

## How It's Calculated

**Cost of Renting:**
Total Rent Cost = Sum of monthly rent (increasing annually by the rent inflation rate) over the holding period, plus the opportunity cost of the down payment that could have been invested.

**Cost of Buying:**
Total Buying Cost = Down Payment + Total mortgage payments (principal + interest) + Property taxes + Home insurance + Maintenance (1% of home value per year) + Closing costs − Home equity built − Home appreciation − Net proceeds if sold (Sale Price − Remaining Mortgage − Selling Costs).

The calculator finds the break-even year — the point at which the cumulative cost of buying becomes less than the cumulative cost of renting — giving you a clear data-driven answer.
