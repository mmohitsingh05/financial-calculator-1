A **loan payoff calculator** shows you the incredible power of extra payments. Whether you have a mortgage, auto loan, student loan, or personal loan, paying even a little extra each month can shave years off your repayment term and save you thousands in interest. This calculator lets you experiment with different extra payment amounts to find the strategy that works best for your budget.

## How to Use This Calculator

Enter your current loan balance, interest rate, and remaining term. Then choose your extra payment strategy — a fixed extra amount each month, a one-time lump sum, or an annual extra payment. The calculator will compare your current payoff schedule against the accelerated schedule, showing your new payoff date, total interest saved, and how much sooner you'll be debt-free.

## How It's Calculated

This calculator runs an iterative month-by-month simulation using standard amortization logic, but each period includes:

- Remaining_Balance_k = Remaining_Balance_(k-1) − Principal_k − Extra_Payment

The simulation stops when Remaining_Balance ≤ 0. The output includes:
- New payoff date (in months) vs. original term
- Interest Saved = Original_Total_Interest − New_Total_Interest

This iterative approach accurately handles any extra payment pattern — fixed monthly amounts, lump sums, or recurring annual payments.
