An **investment calculator** gives you a realistic projection of how your money can grow over time through the power of compounding returns. Whether you have a lump sum to invest, plan to contribute regularly, or both, this tool shows you the potential future value of your investments. Understanding these projections helps you set realistic savings goals and stay motivated to invest consistently.

## How to Use This Calculator

Start by entering your current investment balance (if any). Then enter the amount you plan to contribute regularly and how often (monthly, quarterly, or annually). Enter your expected annual return rate and the number of years you plan to invest. Toggle the inflation adjustment on to see real (inflation-adjusted) values. The calculator will show your projected future value, total contributions, total investment earnings, and a year-by-year growth chart.

## How It's Calculated

The future value combines lump sum growth with the future value of an annuity for recurring contributions:

**FV = P × (1 + r/n)^(n×t) + PMT × [ ((1 + r/n)^(n×t) − 1) / (r/n) ]**

With inflation adjustment toggled on:

**Real FV = FV / (1 + inflation_rate)^t**

This shows your purchasing-power-adjusted returns, giving you a more honest picture of what your future savings will be worth.
