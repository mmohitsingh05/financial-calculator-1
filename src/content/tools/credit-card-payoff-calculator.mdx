A **credit card payoff calculator** is a powerful motivator for getting out of debt. Credit cards typically charge the highest interest rates of any common debt — averaging 20–28% APR — and making only minimum payments can keep you trapped in debt for decades. This calculator shows you exactly how different payment strategies affect your payoff timeline and total interest, so you can choose the approach that works best for you.

## How to Use This Calculator

Enter your current credit card balance, APR (annual interest rate), and your monthly payment amount. You can choose to calculate with the minimum payment only or enter a fixed payment amount. The calculator will show how many months until your balance reaches zero, the total interest paid, and a year-by-year payoff chart. Try different payment amounts to see how much faster you can become debt-free.

## How It's Calculated

Credit cards typically compound daily but bill monthly. This calculator uses a monthly periodic rate approximation:

**i = APR / 12**

For each month:
- Interest_month = Remaining_Balance × i
- Principal_paid_month = Payment − Interest_month
- Remaining_Balance_new = Remaining_Balance − Principal_paid_month

If "minimum payment" is selected, each month's minimum is calculated as:
Minimum Payment = max(Remaining_Balance × min_payment_percentage, flat_minimum_dollar)

The simulation repeats until Remaining_Balance ≤ 0, tracking months to payoff and total interest paid.
