A **salary calculator** helps you understand your income across different time periods and compare job offers on equal footing. Whether you're negotiating a raise, considering a job with a different pay structure, or just trying to understand your hourly worth, this calculator converts between hourly, daily, weekly, biweekly, monthly, and annual figures.

## How to Use This Calculator

Enter your salary in any format — hourly, weekly, monthly, or annual — and the number of hours you work per week. The calculator will instantly convert your pay to all other time periods. You can also factor in overtime hours to see their impact on total earnings. Use the comparison feature to stack two job offers side by side, including benefits like paid time off and expected bonuses.

## How It's Calculated

Simple period conversions:

- Annual = Hourly × Hours_per_week × Weeks_per_year (typically 52)
- Monthly = Annual / 12
- Biweekly = Annual / 26
- Weekly = Annual / 52
- Daily = Annual / (Weeks_per_year × Days_per_week)

With overtime: Overtime Rate = Hourly Rate × 1.5. Weekly Pay with overtime = (40 × Hourly Rate) + (Overtime Hours × Overtime Rate).
