An **hourly to salary calculator** is essential for hourly workers comparing themselves to salaried positions, or for anyone who wants to understand the true annual value of their time. Whether you're negotiating a new job, evaluating a contract position, or just curious about your annual earning power, this calculator gives you quick, accurate conversions.

## How to Use This Calculator

Enter your hourly wage, the average number of hours you work per week, and the number of weeks you work per year (52 for full-time, 50 if you take 2 weeks unpaid). The calculator will instantly show your equivalent annual salary, monthly income, biweekly pay, and weekly pay. You can also reverse the calculation — enter an annual salary to see your equivalent hourly rate.

## How It's Calculated

The core conversion is straightforward:

**Hourly Rate = Annual Salary / (Hours_per_week × Weeks_per_year)**

Or in reverse:

**Annual Salary = Hourly Rate × Hours_per_week × Weeks_per_year**

Standard assumptions use 40 hours/week × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours/year. The calculator allows you to customize both hours per week and weeks per year for accurate results. The overtime toggle calculates: Weekly Pay = (40 × Hourly Rate) + (Overtime Hours × Hourly Rate × 1.5).
